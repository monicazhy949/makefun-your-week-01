One line, 93 characters, summing up a week spent answering everyone except the calendar:

> 79 entries filed, 6 repos deleted, 312 messages answered — and I still forgot what day it is.

## Why it works

- **Three hard numbers, no filler.** 79 entries filed, 6 repos deleted, 312 messages answered — each one is a thing that actually happened, so the line could only be this week.
- **The turn lands on the last clause.** After all that filing, deleting, and replying, the punchline is losing track of the most basic fact of all: what day it is. Busy to the point of disorientation is the week's real story.
- **Nothing padded, nothing missing.** Every word earns its place inside the 100-character limit, and the em dash does the work of a whole second sentence.

Running `python3 main.py` prints the line and also writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
